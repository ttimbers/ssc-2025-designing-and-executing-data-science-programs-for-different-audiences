## Designing and delivering data science programs for different audiences
by Tiffany Timbers

> Oral presentation for the 2025 SSC annual meeting.

Over the past decade, the Statistics and Computer Science Departments at the University of British Columbia (UBC) have collaborated to develop four distinct data science programs for diverse audiences. First was the Professional Master’s of Data Science, designed for post-graduate students without prior data science training who wish to add data skills to their domain expertise. Next, was the Key Capabilities in Data Science certificate program, targeting mature learners seeking elementary data science knowledge. We then introduced the Undergraduate Minor in Data Science, accessible to any UBC undergraduate aiming to acquire data science skills for application in their major specialization. Most recently, we launched the Undergraduate Major Specialization in Data Science for those aspiring to work as data scientists or pursue post-graduate research in this field. In this talk, we will discuss the strategies used to build these programs and the key factors contributing to their success.
